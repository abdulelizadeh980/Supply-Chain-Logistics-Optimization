## 📂 Project Structure & Methodology

### 1. Data Cleaning & Preprocessing
Ensured data integrity by handling missing values and correcting data types. 
* **Anomaly Detection:** Implemented **Z-Score Analysis** to identify and isolate financial outliers in shipping costs.
* **Feature Engineering:** Developed a `DeliveryDuration` metric to track and evaluate shipping efficiency.

### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
Performed deep-dive statistical analysis to uncover hidden operational patterns.
* Analyzed the distribution of shipping costs versus delivery speeds.
* Tested hypotheses regarding regional performance disparities and risk factors.

### 3. Sentiment Analysis (NLP)
Applied Natural Language Processing (NLP) to unstructured customer feedback using the **TextBlob lexicon**.
* **Classification:** Categorized reviews into **Positive, Neutral, and Negative** sentiments.
* **The Voice of Customer:** Generated a **Word Cloud** to visualize key pain points like "Late" and "Damaged" shipments.

### 4. Advanced Data Visualization
Developed high-impact visuals to support executive decision-making:
* **Heatmaps:** To visualize regional sentiment and delivery risk zones.
* **Pareto Charts:** To identify the root causes of logistics delays (the 80/20 rule).
* **Density Plots:** To analyze the concentration of logistics expenses.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights
* **The 5-Day Threshold:** Customer satisfaction drops significantly once delivery time exceeds 5 days.
* **Cost-Speed Paradox:** High shipping costs do not always guarantee faster delivery, indicating carrier selection inefficiencies.
* **Primary Churn Drivers:** "Late" and "Damaged" delivery issues are the leading causes of negative feedback, outweighing price concerns.

---

## 🎯 Strategic Recommendations
1.  **Automated Alerts:** Implement a proactive alert system for shipments crossing a 4-day threshold to mitigate delays.
2.  **Carrier Optimization:** Renegotiate contracts for routes identified as "High Cost, Low Speed" anomalies.
3.  **Customer Recovery:** Use Sentiment Analysis results to trigger automated "Recovery Coupons" for negative delivery experiences.
4.  **Service Standardization:** Align underperforming regions with the efficiency benchmarks of top-performing zones.
